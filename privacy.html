Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026 • Creator Studio & Video Automation Tool

This Privacy Policy explains how Creator Studio & Video Automation ("we", "our", or "the Service"), operated by Nyi Nyi Han, collects, uses, and safeguards information when you connect and manage accounts through social media platforms, including Meta (Facebook Pages), TikTok, and Google (YouTube).

1. Information We Access and Process

We only access data strictly necessary to automate and manage video creation, subtitle burning, and publishing to your authorized channels:

  • Platform Account Identifiers: Public page/channel IDs, account names, and user IDs (such as Facebook Page ID and TikTok Open ID) necessary to direct uploads.
  • Authorization Tokens: OAuth access tokens granted by you through Meta OAuth or TikTok Login to authenticate API publish requests on your behalf.
  • Media Content & Metadata: Video files, custom thumbnails, titles, descriptions, and hashtags scheduled or published by you.

2. How We Use Information

The information accessed is utilized exclusively for the following operational tasks:

  • Publishing and scheduling videos directly to your authorized Facebook Pages and TikTok accounts via official APIs (Meta Graph API and TikTok Content Posting API).
  • Attaching custom 1080p thumbnails and descriptions to your published posts.
  • Monitoring video processing status and upload completion.
Data Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT sell, rent, monetize, or trade any personal data, account credentials, or video assets to third parties or data brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

All authentication credentials and API keys are stored locally on your authorized infrastructure using encrypted credentials files and protected environment variables. We do not store or transmit your access tokens to unauthorized third-party servers.

4. Third-Party Platform Policies

Our Service interacts with third-party APIs. Your use of these platforms is subject to their respective privacy terms:

  • Meta Platforms (Facebook): Meta Privacy Policy
  • TikTok Pte. Ltd.: TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google (YouTube): Google Privacy Policy

5. User Data Deletion Instructions

You have full autonomy over your data and access permissions at any time:

  • Meta (Facebook): Navigate to Settings & Privacy > Settings > Apps and Websites, locate "Creator Studio / Video Automation", and click Remove. This immediately invalidates all active tokens.
  • TikTok: Open the TikTok App, go to Profile > Settings and Privacy > Security > Manage App Permissions, and revoke access for this tool.
  • Complete Data Erasure: To request instant manual deletion of any cached identifiers or logs, email us at nyinyihan17@gmail.com. Requests are completed within 24 hours. Detailed instructions are also available at our User Data Deletion Page.
