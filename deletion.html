User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 24, 2026 • Meta & TikTok Compliance

In accordance with Meta Platform Rules and TikTok Developer Policies, Creator Studio & Video Automation provides simple, immediate mechanisms for users to delete their account data and revoke access tokens.

Option 1: Revoking Facebook / Meta Permissions

  1. Go to your Facebook Account Settings: Facebook Apps & Websites.
  2. Under Active Apps and Websites, find Creator Studio / Video Automation.
  3. Click Remove. Facebook will immediately revoke all access tokens granted to our application.
  4. Check the box to request that Facebook delete all historical interactions, posts, or links shared through the app if desired.

Option 2: Revoking TikTok Permissions

  1. Open the TikTok App on your mobile device.
  2. Go to Profile > tap the 3-line menu > Settings and Privacy.
  3. Tap Security > Manage App Permissions.
  4. Select Creator Studio and tap Remove Access.

Option 3: Request Direct Manual Data Erasure

If you wish for us to manually purge any temporary log entries, cached identifiers, or configuration tokens associated with your account from our server/workspace, send an email to:
